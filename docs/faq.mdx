## Why is the working-day count lower than the date range?

Weekends, non-working weekdays and public holidays for the employee's country are excluded. Half days count as 0.5.

## Who receives my request?

Your direct manager first. If none is set, the approver for your office. If neither exists, a superadmin receives it.

## Can colleagues see why I am away?

No. Regular employees see your name and approved dates on the team calendar. Leave type, note, document and decision history stay private.

## What happens when I change approved leave?

You submit an amendment. The original booking stays approved until an approver accepts the new dates.

## Why does my leave year not end in December?

Your country may use an employment-anniversary leave year. The reset date then follows your start date rather than the calendar year.

## Does work from home reduce annual leave?

Only when your company configures that leave type to deduct from an allowance. In the standard setup it is tracked but unlimited.

## Can I cancel approved leave?

Yes. Cancelling removes it from the calendar, restores any deducted days and notifies the approver.

## What is the difference between deactivation and blocked sign-in?

Deactivation means the person is no longer active staff. Blocking sign-in keeps them active in headcount and leave records but prevents account access.

## Does the public demo send email?

No. The demo contains no email delivery credentials. In-app notifications still work, and the workspace resets every night.
