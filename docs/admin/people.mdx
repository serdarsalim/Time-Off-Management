**Employees** is the company directory for superadmins. Open a person to manage the details that affect leave calculations and approval routing.

## Employment details

The record keeps country, office, department, direct manager, start date, job title and active status. Country and start date matter: they can change the person's leave period, statutory entitlement and public holidays.

## Leave and lateness

The same record combines allowance balances, leave history, amendment history and late arrivals. Managers can add a private note that only authorised approvers can see.

## Access controls

- **Deactivate** removes the person from active headcount and prevents sign-in.
- **Block sign-in** keeps them active in the organisation but revokes account access.
- **Hide from roster** keeps a company account out of shared staff views without turning it into an employee.

Use the employee's account menu to act as them when you need to verify their exact navigation and data scope.
