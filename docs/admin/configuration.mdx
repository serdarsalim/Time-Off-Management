## Country rules

Each country has a leave-year mode:

- **Calendar year** runs from January to December and prorates the joining year.
- **Employment anniversary** resets on each person's start-date anniversary and can apply a first-period allowance and waiting period.

## Leave types

A leave type has a name, colour, annual allowance, whether it deducts from the balance, whether a document can be attached and how many unused statutory days may carry over. Drag leave types to change their display order.

## Public holidays and working days

Public holidays are stored by country and year. They appear on the calendar and do not count as requested working days. Configure the normal working weekdays for teams whose week is not Monday to Friday.

## Calendar visibility

Choose whether regular employees see approved leave for the whole company or only their own office. The setting changes names and dates only. Private leave details remain protected either way.
