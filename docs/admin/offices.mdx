---
title: "Offices and departments"
description: "Route approvals by office and organise people by function"
---

Time Off uses two different organisational fields:

- **Office** controls default approval routing. Each office can have one approver.
- **Department** is a functional label such as Admissions, Marketing or Finance. It does not grant access or route approvals.

Open **Offices** to see headcount, who is off now, who is coming up in the next 14 days and the assigned approver. Names save inline when you finish editing.

<Note>
A person's direct manager wins over the office approver. The office approver is the fallback when no direct manager is set.
</Note>
