---
title: "Tour the demo"
description: "A ten-minute walkthrough of the public Time Off workspace"
---

The [live demo](https://time-off.autotakt.dev) opens as a superadmin. It contains 12 fictional employees, two offices, leave history, pending requests and reports. Changes are safe and the data resets nightly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the dashboard">
    Start with **Dashboard**. The top cards show pending approvals, who is off today, who is off this week and active headcount. The tables below show decisions waiting for you, upcoming absences and allowance balances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a leave request">
    Open **My Leave** and choose **Request leave**. Pick a leave type and dates, try a half day, then submit. Because the demo user is a superadmin, you can also use the employee switcher to act as a fictional colleague.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a decision">
    Open **Approvals**. Expand any pending request to see dates, working-day count, balance impact and the employee's note. Approve or decline it and watch the dashboard and calendar update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check team coverage">
    Open **Team Calendar**. Move between months or the full-year view. Colleagues see names and dates, while private leave types and notes stay hidden unless they are allowed to approve that person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an employee record">
    Open **Employees**, then choose a name. The record combines employment details, allowance balances, leave history, late arrivals and private manager notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the rules">
    Open **Time-off configuration** to see the Ireland and Türkiye policies. Each country owns its leave types, public holidays, working days, first-year rule and carryover limit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Email delivery is disabled in the public demo. In-app notifications and every data change still follow the production path.
</Note>
