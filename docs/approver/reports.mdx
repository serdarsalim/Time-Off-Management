---
title: "Reports"
description: "Review leave taken, remaining balances and team patterns"
---

Reports are available to office managers, admins and superadmins. They follow the same people scope as approvals.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reports.png" alt="Time Off reports with allowance and leave totals" />
</Frame>

Filter by country, office, employee, leave type or date period. The report separates entitlement from use so you can answer both operational questions, such as who is away next month, and balance questions, such as who still has substantial annual leave left.

Numbers use the person's own leave period. An Ireland employee usually resets on 31 December. A Türkiye employee can reset on their employment anniversary.
