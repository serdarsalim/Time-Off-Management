**Approvals** contains pending leave and pending amendments for the people you manage. Superadmins see every pending item in the company.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/approvals.png" alt="Approvals queue with requests waiting for a decision" />

## Review a request

Expand a row to see the leave type, date range, working-day count, note and balance impact. Open the employee's name when you need their full leave history or allowance breakdown.

Approve when the dates and coverage work. Decline when they do not, and add a short decision note so the employee knows what to change.

## Review an amendment

An amendment shows both the currently approved booking and the proposed dates. Approving replaces the old booking. Declining keeps the original dates and records your note.

## Decision scope

The system checks scope on every read and write. Hiding a page is not the security boundary. An office manager cannot decide a request outside their team by opening a copied link.
