The dashboard changes with your role. Employees see their own balances and upcoming leave. Approvers also see requests that need a decision and a team allowance table.

## Availability cards

- **Off today** counts approved leave covering today.
- **Off this week** counts people with approved leave during the current week.
- **Active staff** excludes offboarded people and accounts hidden from the roster.
- **Pending approvals** appears when your role can decide requests.

## Your leave

Each allowance card shows the amount left in the current leave period. Paid leave shows the total and remaining days. Non-deducting types, such as work from home, show days taken without imposing a limit.

## Team allowance table

Approvers can switch country and compare entitlement, used days, remaining days, tenure and the date each person's leave period resets. Use the column control to keep only the measures you need.
