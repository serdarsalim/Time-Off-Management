---
title: "Log a late arrival"
description: "Record a late arrival without changing leave balances"
---

A late arrival is an informational record, not leave. From the dashboard, choose **Log late arrival**, enter the date, how late you were and the reason.

Your manager receives an in-app notification. They can opt into email alerts from **Account**. The entry appears on your employee record and does not reduce any allowance.

<Note>
Use a leave request when the time away should count against an allowance. Use a late-arrival entry when the team only needs a record of when you arrived and why.
</Note>
