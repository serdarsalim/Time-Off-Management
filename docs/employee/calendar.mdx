The team calendar contains approved leave and public holidays. Use the month controls for day-to-day planning or switch to the year view for a wider picture.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calendar.png" alt="Team calendar showing approved leave and public holidays" />
</Frame>

## What colleagues see

Regular employees see the person's name and dates. They do not see the leave type, note, supporting document or decision history. Approvers see more only for people they are allowed to manage.

## Company or office visibility

A superadmin chooses the calendar scope in **Time-off configuration**:

- **Company** shows approved leave across the whole company.
- **Office** limits employees to colleagues in their own office.

Public holidays follow the employee's configured country. They do not reduce a leave balance and are excluded from working-day counts.
