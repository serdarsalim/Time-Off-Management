Time Off keeps employee leave in one shared system. Employees can request time off, see what they have left and check the team calendar. Approvers can make decisions, review absence patterns and manage the people they are responsible for.

## Built for a cross-border team

Time Off supports calendar-year and employment-anniversary leave periods in the same workspace. A person's country decides which public holidays, working days and statutory leave rules apply. Ireland and Türkiye are configured in the demo, and a superadmin can add another supported country from **Time-off configuration**.

## See it live

Open the [public demo](https://time-off.autotakt.dev) to explore a fictional team in Dublin and Istanbul. There is no signup. The workspace resets every night, so you can approve, reject, edit and reassign without affecting a real person.
